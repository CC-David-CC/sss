## Reproduce within an authorized environment

Follow [START-HERE](../../docs/START-HERE.md) for the installed-package/reference
walkthrough and [OPERATOR-HANDOFF](../../docs/OPERATOR-HANDOFF.md) for Linux,
external-application, archive and native-lesson commands. Follow
[BW-WORKFLOWS](../../docs/BW-WORKFLOWS.md) for operator-prepared domain runtimes.
Preserve original run directories; every new experiment needs a new directory.
Private receipts and their hashes remain with the operator. Protected cases,
raw traces, credentials, models and the bulk archive are excluded from Git.
