independently checked synthetic practice. Finished means the example artifact is
complete, not that Wen was trained on it. The research demonstration includes
multiple rounds, archived evidence and a delayed reread after a controlled
omission from the working note. Its reference byte-token counts are not Wen

not captured private thoughts from a language model. Selecting target labels is
